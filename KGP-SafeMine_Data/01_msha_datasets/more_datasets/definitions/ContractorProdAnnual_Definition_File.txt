TABLE_NAME|COLUMN_NAME|DATA_TYPE|DATA_LENGTH|FIELD_DESCRIPTION
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|CONTRACTOR_ID|VARCHAR2|7|Identification number assigned by MSHA for a legal entity acting as a contractor at a mine.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|CONTRACTOR_NAME|VARCHAR2|60|The name of the contractor that reported this information.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|CAL_YR|NUMBER|4|The 4-digit year on which the record is summarized.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|SUBUNIT_CD|VARCHAR2|2|Code that identifies the location/operation within a mine.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|SUBUNIT|VARCHAR2|32|Narrative description of each subunit code: (01) Underground;  (02) Surface at underground;  (03) Strip, quarry, open pit;  (04) Auger;  (05) Culm bank/refuse pile;  (06) Dredge;  (12) Other mining;  (17) Independent shops or yards;  (30) Mill operation/preparation plant;  (99) Office workers at mine site.  
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|ANNUAL_COAL_PRODUCTION|NUMBER|9|The annual summation of coal production where the average quarterly employment is greater than zero with grouping by calendar year, subunit code and contractor ID.  
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|ANNUAL_HOURS|NUMBER|9|The annual summation of hours where the average quarterly employment is greater than zero with grouping by calendar year, subunit code and contractor ID.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|AVG_EMPLOYEE_CNT|NUMBER|9|The average of the quarterly average employee count where the average quarterly employment is greater than zero and is grouped by calendar year, subunit code and contractor ID.
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|AVG_EMPLOYEE_HOURS|NUMBER|7|Average employee hours derived by the following:  hours reported for year (annual_hours) / avg subunit empl for year (avg_employee_cnt).
ANNUAL CONTRACTOR EMPLOY/PROD|COAL_METAL_IND|VARCHAR2|1|Identifies if the employment and production are being reported for a Coal or Metal/Non-Metal mine.
